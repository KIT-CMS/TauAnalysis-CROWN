ERA-TAG DECORRELATION PASS — analysis_configurations/tau/config.py
====================================================================
Branch: audit-run3-htautau-eratags (nested TauAnalysis-CROWN repo)
Scope: tasks "use era tag for uncorrelated uncertainties as indicated in
the twikis of the objects", "decorrelate z pt uncertainties per era", and
"tau variation uncorrelated by era per the TauPOG (non-CMS-TWiki) reference".

Sources used: systematics_master.txt, Conventions_-_CMS_Common_Analysis_
Tools_Documentation.txt, Introduction_-_TauPOG_Wiki.txt, DY_kinematic_

WHAT WAS DONE
--------------
Added a module-level ERA_MAP (matching jet_variations.py's ERA_MAP /
systematics_master.yml era tokens: 2022preEE->2022, 2022postEE->2022EE,
2023preBPix->2023, 2023postBPix->2023BPix, else identity) and
`era_tag = ERA_MAP[era]` at the top of build_config. Embedded era_tag
into the shift `name=` string (not the correctionlib key/shift_map, which
stays unchanged) for every uncorrelated-by-era uncertainty I could
confirm against either an explicit era regex group in systematics_master
or an explicit "uncorrelated across years/eras" sentence in the TauPOG
Wiki:

  muonEsScale, muonEsReso, eleEsReso, eleEsScale, electronIdSF, PileUp,
  zPtReweightWeight_unc{n} (both NNLO/powheg and NLO/amcatnlo loops),
  tauEsDM{dm}, tauEsDM{dm}pT{pt}, tauEleFakeEsDM{dm}Barrel/Endcap,
  tauMuFakeEsDM{dm}, vsEleDM{dm}Barrel/Endcap (era>=2022), vsMuWheel{wheel}
  (era>=2022 branch only).

Also fixed vsJetStat1/2{era}DM{dm}, vsJetSystTES{era}DM{dm}, and
vsJetSyst{era} (added by a prior tau-SF audit) to use the mapped era_tag
token instead of the raw era string, so "2022postEE" becomes "2022EE" in
the shift name, matching every other era-tagged shift in this codebase
and the systematics_master token convention. This is a spelling fix only
— era was already embedded, so decorrelation behavior is unchanged.

Full details, including exact rationale/twiki citation per shift and a
list of what was deliberately NOT touched (Run2 vsEle/vsMu names, MET
recoil naming, prefiring source ambiguity, CMS_htt_zptReweight's missing
CADI id, and the fact that the CMS_* prefix rename itself was not applied
to any of these — only era_tag suffixes were added to the existing legacy
names), are appended to shift_rename_log.txt under "Follow-up pass:
era-tag decorrelation".
